Support

AhamMaxxing — an app for setting targets and checking them off.

Something not working, or not making sense? Email anupmas@gmail.com and describe what you did and what happened. There is no ticket system; a person reads it.

Common questions

Where is my data?

In a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive, under Apps / AhamMaxxing. Open it any time — it is an ordinary Google Sheet, and it is readable without the app. Full detail on the privacy page.

Why does it ask me to sign in to Google?

That is the only way it can write to your Drive. The app asks for the narrowest permission Google offers: access to files this app itself created, and nothing else in your Drive.

I signed in and my targets are gone.

Check you signed in with the same Google account as before. The data lives with the account, not with the phone, so a different account starts empty by design.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google account and both read the same spreadsheet.

How do I start over?

Settings has Start again, which leaves your old spreadsheet in Drive untouched and begins a new one. If you want the old data gone as well, delete that spreadsheet in Drive yourself.

The daily reminder does not arrive.

Check that notifications are allowed for AhamMaxxing in the iPhone's Settings → Notifications. Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself, so they also need the app to have been opened at least once since the reminder time was set.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the spreadsheet in your Google Drive and remove the app. Nothing is left anywhere else — there is no server holding a copy. You can also revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Reporting a problem

Useful things to include: what you tapped, what you expected, what happened instead, and whether you were on the iPhone app or the website. A screenshot helps more than a description.
